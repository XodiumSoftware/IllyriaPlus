# Spawn Protection

The area around the Overworld spawn is a safe zone, sized by the server's vanilla
`spawn-protection` setting.

## What is protected <img class="mc-icon" alt="" src="../img/item/villager_spawn_egg.png">

- **Players** are invulnerable to all damage — mobs, PvP, fall damage, fire, explosions, anything
- **Villagers** and **tamed animals** (pets) are invulnerable to all damage too

## Monster-free zone

- Monsters cannot spawn inside the protected area
- Monsters that wander in or load with a chunk are automatically despawned

Outside the protected radius everything behaves as normal survival Minecraft.
