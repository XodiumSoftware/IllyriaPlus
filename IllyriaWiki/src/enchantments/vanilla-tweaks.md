# Vanilla Enchantment Tweaks

IllyriaRPG adjusts the behavior of several vanilla enchantments.

## Fortune — Auto-Replant <img class="mc-icon" alt="" src="../img/item/wheat.png">

When breaking a fully-grown crop with a hoe enchanted with **Fortune II or higher**, the crop is automatically
replanted.

| Level       | Auto-Replant |
| ----------- | ------------ |
| Fortune I   | No           |
| Fortune II  | Yes          |
| Fortune III | Yes          |

## Feather Falling

Adjusted fall protection behavior.

## Silk Touch <img class="mc-icon" alt="" src="../img/item/pickaxe.webp">

Extended silk touch capabilities for additional block types.
