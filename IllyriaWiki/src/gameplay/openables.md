# Openable Blocks

Extended behavior for doors and other openable blocks.

## Features

| Feature           | How to Trigger                              |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Double Door Sync  | Right-click either door of a double door pair |
| Door Knocking     | Sneak + left-click a door with an empty hand |

---

## Double Door Sync

Right-clicking one door of a **double door pair** automatically opens or closes the adjacent door in sync.
No more manually clicking both doors.

### Notes

- Only works when right-clicking a door normally (not while sneaking)
- The adjacent door must mirror the clicked door (hinge on the opposite side)

---

## Door Knocking

Announce your arrival by knocking on a door!

### How to Knock

1. **Empty your hand** (hold nothing)
2. **Sneak** (hold Shift)
3. **Left-click** any door, trapdoor, or fence gate

A knocking sound plays to all players within **10 blocks**.

Works in Survival and Adventure modes on doors, trapdoors, and fence gates — anything openable.
