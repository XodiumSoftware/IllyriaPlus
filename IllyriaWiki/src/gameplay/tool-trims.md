# Tool Trims <img class="mc-icon" alt="" src="../img/item/netherite_sword.png">

All vanilla armor trims can be applied to tools — swords, pickaxes, axes, shovels, and
hoes of every tier — in a smithing table.

## How It Works

Place any **armor trim smithing template**, a **tool**, and any **trim material**
(iron ingot, gold ingot, diamond, etc.) into a smithing table. The tool comes out with
the chosen trim applied — the trim tints the item sprite and appears in the tooltip.

## Notes

- All **vanilla armor trims and materials** work on tools of all six tiers (wooden,
  stone, iron, golden, diamond, netherite).
- Trimming a tool is **cosmetic only** — durability, damage, and mining speed are unchanged.
- Durability carries over when trimming — the smithing table does not reset damage.
- Templates are the same items used for armor trims — find them in their usual structures
  (shipwrecks, strongholds, trial chambers, etc.).
