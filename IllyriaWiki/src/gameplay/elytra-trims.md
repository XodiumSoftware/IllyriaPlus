# Elytra Trims <img class="mc-icon" alt="" src="../img/item/elytra.png">

Elytra can be armor-trimmed in a smithing table, just like chestplates. Trims appear both
on the item icon and on the wings when worn.

## How It Works

Place an **Elytra**, any **Armor Trim Smithing Template**, and any **trim material**
(iron ingot, gold ingot, diamond, etc.) into a smithing table. The elytra comes out with
the chosen trim applied — the trim texture renders on the wings when worn and tints the
item sprite.

## Notes

- All **vanilla armor trims and materials** work on elytra.
- Trimming an elytra is **cosmetic only** — protection, gliding, and durability are unchanged.
- Trims show on **broken elytra** too, via a separate item sprite.
- Durability carries over when trimming — the smithing table does not reset damage.
