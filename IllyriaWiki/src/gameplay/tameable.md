# Pet Transfer

Give a tamed pet to another player.

## How to Transfer Ownership

1. Equip a **Lead** in your main hand.
2. Leash a pet you have already tamed (so the lead is attached to it).
3. **Right-click the receiving player** while still holding the lead.

The pet's owner changes to the target player and the lead reattaches to them automatically.

## Notes

- The pet must currently be leashed **by you** — a pet leashed to a fence post or to another
  player cannot be transferred.
- Only the current owner can give the pet away; you cannot steal someone else's pet.
- The receiving player must be online and within reach of the leash.
- Ownership transfers are permanent until the new owner transfers the pet back.
- Works for any tamed animal — wolves, cats, horses, parrots, etc. — as long as it can be leashed.
