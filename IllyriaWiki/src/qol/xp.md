# XP Management

Store your experience points in bottles for safekeeping.

## Bottling XP

**Right-click an enchanting table** with an empty **glass bottle** to convert your XP into an
**Experience Bottle**.

## Why Bottle XP?

- **Protect your levels** — bottled XP survives death
- **Trading** — hand XP to other players as bottles
- **Storage** — save XP for later enchanting sessions
